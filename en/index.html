How it works

  1. Describe the device and fault online, by phone or by email.
  2. We inspect the device and explain the practical options.
  3. You decide after we have discussed the work and costs.
  4. We carry out the agreed repair, test the device and return it.

Questions

Can I come without an appointment?

Yes. Contacting us first helps us plan enough time for your device.

How long does a repair take?

It depends on the fault and availability of parts. We give you a realistic estimate after assessment.

Will my data be kept confidential?

We handle devices and stored data carefully and confidentially. Please back up important files where possible; data preservation cannot be guaranteed.

Is data recovery guaranteed?

No. The relevant storage areas must still exist and be readable. Fully overwritten data cannot be restored by this service.

Does an enquiry commit me to a repair?

No. Sending the form is only an enquiry. Paid work starts after your approval.
