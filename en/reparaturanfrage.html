Directions & device handover

PC & Handyservice Augsburg – Maurice Keil
Stätzlinger Straße 99, 86165 Augsburg

Commercial repair service based in a private residential building. I welcome you personally at the courtyard entrance for device drop-off and collection by confirmed appointment.

Google Maps initially takes you to the front of the large white building at Stätzlinger Straße 99, by the bus stop and pizzeria. Facing the building from the street, follow Eppaner Straße along its left side. After about 30 metres, turn right behind the building into the large courtyard with many parked cars. Our residential entrance is at the far end of the courtyard, marked by our business sign and labelled doorbell.

Device drop-off and collection are by prior, confirmed appointment only. A repair enquiry alone is not an appointment confirmation.

We meet you at the entrance for the handover. There is no publicly accessible shop.

For the short handover (about 5–15 minutes), please park directly beside the fence opposite the building entrance, where permitted. Keep access routes and passageways clear and follow the signs. Leave the areas to the left and right of the entrance, and the side and marked parking spaces, free. Alternatively, use permitted parking on the main road.

Please treat our neighbours respectfully, avoid unnecessary noise and do not ring other residents’ doorbells.

If you cannot find the entrance, please call: 0152 54530080

I look forward to your repair enquiry and to meeting you in person. Whether it is a computer, smartphone, tablet or console, tell me briefly what you need help with. Together we will discuss the most suitable support for your device.
